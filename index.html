Meet Randie

A Voice Built From Real Life

Randie O’Neil writes the kind of songs that stay with you long after the final note.

Rooted in Americana, folk-pop, and fearless storytelling, her music reflects resilience, hard-earned wisdom, and the courage to tell the truth without hiding behind it.

Every lyric carries a piece of lived experience. Every performance invites the listener into a story that feels personal, honest, and unmistakably her own.
